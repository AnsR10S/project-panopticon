# Scientific and operational limitations

- Public receipt announcements are an operational manipulation, not proof of
  formal common knowledge. Private recipients may infer common receipt.
- Live alert timing responds to behavior. Event-study before/after comparisons
  alone do not identify the effect of warnings.
- The placebo schedule is independent, but may differ in timing, salience,
  frequency and information. Four arms do not identify every mechanism.
- High prices, aligned prices, or a behavioral flag do not establish intent or
  an agreement. The auditor is not a calibrated truth detector.
- V1 validates one three-firm linear differentiated market. Generalization to
  other markets/models/welfare measures requires separate validation.
- Returned text is a visible explanation, not privileged access to internal
  reasoning. No activation or weight monitoring is implemented.
- A fixed seed does not guarantee API determinism. Scripted reproducibility
  excludes timestamp/duration metadata from its semantic comparison.
- No live provider/model compatibility, strategic emergence, cost pilot,
  confirmatory power, novelty claim, or empirical paradox result is established.
- Configured-rate cost reservations are estimates. Provider usage, prices, and
  billing conventions need verification before paid studies.
- Crash-safe API resume and exactly-once external execution are not provided.
  Preserve failed attempts and restart under a fresh identity.
- Local hashes detect corruption and semantic inconsistency; they are not
  externally signed provenance or protection against rewriting every artifact.
