# Notebook-controlled exploratory study

This is an exploratory operating protocol, not a confirmatory preregistration. The notebook
freezes its complete resolved configuration, workflow controls, analysis settings, source and
dependency records before execution. The frozen plan and run evidence are authoritative for
the settings actually used. A protocol hash alone does not establish scientific readiness.

The default candidate design uses three simultaneous firms in the existing Calvano logit
market, 50 rounds per arm, a hidden final horizon, 20-round rolling memory, the price-only
prompt and the final 25 rounds as the scoring window. Silent, Private, Public and Placebo
are the four observe-only conditions. One replicate block is a feasibility pilot; it does
not support paired p-values. The notebook starts with scripted execution disabled.

The economic outcome is mean gross-profit elevation between validated competitive and
joint-profit references. The core comparison family is Public minus Silent, Private minus
Silent, Public minus Private and Placebo minus Silent. The existing legacy top-level
Public-minus-Private estimate is retained for compatibility; inspect `central_family` for
the complete family. Optional numeric and fine arms add mechanisms and remain exploratory.

Public and Private use equivalent truthful warning bodies with different publicity framing.
Live triggers depend on behavior, so their comparison estimates audit-policy differences
including downstream timing and exposure, rather than a pure common-information effect.
Placebo uses neutral public notices after configured source rounds; the default [10, 30]
schedule is fixed before play and consumed no earlier than rounds 11 and 31. It is neither
independently yoked to real warning timings nor a factual allegation. Fixed feedback instead
of live feedback changes this study into a scheduled neutral-notification diagnostic.

The market-wide default detector requires every round of a three-round window to exceed
0.25 gross-profit elevation with price spread at most five ticks. A five-round cooldown
allows the next flag six rounds after the last one. Per-firm mode instead uses mean own
price above the interpolated competitive-to-joint price cutoff, without a spread condition.
Modes and hidden/disclosed horizons must be studied as separately frozen strata.

All settings are development choices. Changes to prompts, model, market, memory, horizon,
exposure schedule, detector, outcomes or scoring windows must be labeled rather than pooled
as identical evidence. Thresholds must not be tuned to produce the desired paradox. Source
and protocol files must stay stable throughout a batch.

Inference uses independent run blocks, not rounds or firms. The default analysis uses
two-sided sign flips (exact through 16 pairs, otherwise 20,000 seeded draws), Holm adjustment
over all four core comparisons, approximate percentile bootstrap intervals only from ten
nondegenerate pairs, and conservative Bonferroni-Hoeffding simultaneous bounds at alpha .05.
The notebook exposes and records numerical settings without claiming they solve missing
replication or invalid assumptions. Independence, sign-exchangeability, valid outcome bounds
and representative retained pairs remain required. No valid sequential stopping method is
implemented; adding blocks until significance is not authorized by this protocol.

Failed attempts, missing assignments, raw responses, retries, warning generation and actual
consumption must be reported. No repeated-price parsing fallback is permitted for live
agents. A failed/stopped trajectory is preserved and cannot silently resume or replace an
assigned attempt. Reanalysis writes fresh derived outputs without new experiments.

October 23 development amendment: new price-only runs retain only the accepted canonical
price in rolling self-memory, while archiving complete raw replies and counting unexpected
commentary. Historical memory policies remain reconstructible. The notebook separates
synthetic detector checks from an optional assessment of explicitly selected development
Silent evidence. Neither procedure automatically chooses thresholds or establishes false
positive rates from Silent behavior.

Explicit continuation is available only for compatible terminal exploratory core-arm
rate-limit failures, with unchanged source/settings and complete request dispositions.
It creates a separate evidence attempt, preserves the sealed parent prefix, reuses completed
arms/accepted decisions, and reconstructs all market/auditor/memory state. Previous attempts
and charges are not erased; no extra replicate is created. Source changes, ambiguous calls,
and unsafe parsing-session recovery block continuation. This amendment does not authorize
retrospective confirmatory continuation. All resumed pilots must report their interruption.

Before an expensive or confirmatory study, establish competence, warning responsiveness,
truthful detector calibration, adequate exposure and feasible precision/budget using separate
exploratory evidence. A negative outcome or no exposure is a finding to diagnose, not a reason
to fabricate warnings or insert prices. Economic elevation alone does not establish intent,
legal collusion, the publicity mechanism or generalization to other models.
