# Auditor's Paradox V2 exploratory protocol

This protocol supports implementation verification and separately labeled development evidence. It is not a confirmatory preregistration. The [approved design](../design/2026-10-23-v2/README.md) records the rationale and decisions.

Three firms choose simultaneously from the configured grid. All firms receive completed public prices and their own accounting; no current rival choice, private note, future schedule or hidden horizon is supplied. V2 memory contains the declared rolling outcome window and, only in the explicit private-note profile, the latest bounded own note. Model weights are not trained.

The warning-only family is Public-Silent, Private-Silent, Public-Private and Placebo-Silent, all primary when prospectively frozen, with two-sided sign-flip inference under its assumptions and Holm family-wise alpha .05. Placebo V2 is an active uncertainty-signal control. Legacy neutral wording retains its original identity.

Sanctions use separate event and continuing study identities. Fine Only, Private Fine and Public Fine share their initial financial instructions and own gross/deduction/net accounting. Their contrasts are Fine Only-Silent, Private Fine-Fine Only, Public Fine-Fine Only and Public Fine-Private Fine. These are different regime effects, not a pure transfer effect.

Qualification precedes cooldown. Consecutive qualifying assessments select declared severity tiers, resetting after qualification fails. Event deductions require a cooldown-eligible event; continuing deductions apply on every qualifying assessment, including onset, independently of announcement cooldown. Deduction equals declared rate times positive gross profit; net equals gross minus deduction. The initial 10/20/40% tiers are diagnostic choices, not validated deterrence parameters.

The primary endpoint is final-window mean gross-profit elevation against validated competitive and joint-profit references. Prices, dispersion, temporal variability, cumulative gross/net profits, deduction frequency, warning generation/delivery/consumption and costs are secondary. Breakdown is the configured number of consecutive nonqualifying assessments after an established episode; unfinished episodes are censored.

Manual interventions pause after a market commit and before the next observations. The worker previews and accepts exact messages, records immutable provenance, and delivers only at t+1. Manual changes never reverse prices or deductions. Accepted manual interventions mark a run researcher-intervened and exclude it from untouched research contrasts. Interactive interventions are disabled for confirmatory runs.

Raw evidence is authoritative. SQLite is a reconstructible index. Failed/stopped attempts and incomplete decisions are retained; unknown usage is not zero. Continuations retain the original assignment and costs. Incompatible source/settings or unknown in-flight calls block recovery. Independent trajectories, not rounds or firms, are replicates. Inference cannot manufacture missing independent evidence.

Warning-only development initially retains 50 rounds/tail 25; sanction development may use 30/tail 15 with a separate identity. Model/prompt selection uses competence and reliability rather than elevated prices. Final meaningful effect, duration, sample size, failure rules and protocol must be prospectively frozen using independent development evidence. A $1,000 project resource ceiling provides no authorization for paid requests.
